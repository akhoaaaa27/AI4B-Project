This is an actual anatomical hand mesh with a natural, slightly curved open pose, not a hand assembled from primitives. It is a lightweight WebXR asset (1,360 vertices, 2,314 triangles; 80,936 bytes), with no skin texture, no animations and no skeleton in this fixed-pose version. The prototype replaces its original neutral material with a smooth graphite/teal sculpture material and transforms the entire model; the source geometry is unmodified.

It is suitable as a semi-realistic digital sculpture for a visual prototype. It is **not a high-resolution photorealistic hand scan**. A commissioned sculpt or licensed high-detail posed hand would be needed for close-up cinematic production quality.
